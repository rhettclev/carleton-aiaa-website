Help us build and fly

No Canadian team has ever won AIAA's Design/Build/Fly competition. That's the goal Carleton's team is chasing this season — and sponsorship is what turns it from a design on paper into a competition-ready aircraft.

Who we are

A student-led capstone project, backed by the whole Student Branch.

The Carleton DBF Team is a student-led capstone project competing in the 31st annual AIAA Design/Build/Fly competition. The team is primarily ten fourth-year capstone students in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, supported by underclassmen who contribute to design, manufacturing, testing, and outreach throughout the season.

DBF is one of the primary technical activities run through the Carleton AIAA Student Branch, operating under the joint support of Carleton's Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAAE) Capstone Program and the Student Branch itself — which is currently growing its student participation, industry engagement, and range of aerospace activities on campus.

The competition

One of the world's premier collegiate aircraft design competitions — and one no Canadian team has won yet.
