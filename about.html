Your aerospace community at Carleton

The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) connects students, engineers, and aerospace professionals worldwide. Our Student Branch brings that network directly to Carleton.

We're building a central community where students from different programs, years, and interests come together around one thing: aerospace. Whether you're into aircraft, drones, rockets, spacecraft, propulsion, structures, avionics, controls, manufacturing, research, business, or policy — there's a place for you here.

You don't need to already be part of a design team, and you don't need years of technical experience.
