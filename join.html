How it works

  1. Fill out the interest form

    Scan the QR code or use the button — takes under a minute.

  2. Get your AIAA membership

    $37 USD/year, paid directly to AIAA. This is what makes you an official Student Branch member.

  3. Reach out to us personally

    Email us or message @aiaa.carleton on Instagram — we like to actually talk to new members, not just add them to a list.
