Frequently asked questions

The short answers. If yours isn't here, reach out and we'll help directly.

How do I join?

Use the get involved link or QR code on the Join page to fill out our interest form. From there, we'll follow up with next steps and information about AIAA membership.

Do I need experience to join?

No. We have roles and events for all skill levels and all programs — you don't need to already be part of a design team, and you don't need to be in engineering specifically.

How much does membership cost?

AIAA University Student Membership is $37 USD per year, paid directly to AIAA. It covers your membership both in the Carleton branch and AIAA's global network.
